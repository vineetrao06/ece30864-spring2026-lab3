b) The product owner defines the vision (such as creating the goal, strategy, etc) while also maintaining the backlog. 
The scrum master, on the other hand, coaches the team on Scrum rules and gets rid of any type of blocker/distraction. Overall, both are essential in different ways in a corporate setting. 

c) The three types of meetings during a spring cycle are daily standup, spring planning, and spring review/retrospective. 
For sprint planing, the team sets a goal and chooses which tasks from the product backlog to tackle. In the daily standup, team members share what they finished yesterday, what they will do today, and any roadblocks. In the spring review/retrospective, the team shows completed work to stakeholders for feedback (the review) and discusses how to improve their teamwork and processes (the retrospective).
